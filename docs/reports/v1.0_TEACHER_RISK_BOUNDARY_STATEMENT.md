# STAMP v1.0 — Risk and Scientific Boundary Statement

**For:** IRB protocols, thesis committees, collaboration agreements  
**Date:** 2026-05-12  
**Version:** v1.0.0

---

## 1. Platform Origin and Purpose

STAMP (Synthetic anti-infective Assisted by Machine-learning Peptide design) was developed by a researcher with a veterinary medicine background transitioning into AI-assisted peptide design. The platform integrates computational biology tools (BepiPred3, PepMLM, ColabFold, MMPBSA) into a unified workflow with strict scientific boundary enforcement.

**Purpose:** Accelerate the design-evaluation-feedback loop for targeted anti-infective peptides while preventing common pitfalls in computational drug discovery: over-interpretation of in-silico scores, fabrication of experimental data, and premature claims of therapeutic efficacy.

---

## 2. Computational vs. Experimental Distinction

STAMP makes the following distinctions explicit in code, UI, and documentation:

| Category | Examples | Status in v1.0 |
|---|---|---|
| **Computational predictions** | pLDDT, pDockQ, FoldX energy, composite_score, BepiPred3 score, PepMLM output, MM-GBSA ΔTOTAL (pilot) | Available, clearly labeled "in-silico" |
| **Production binding affinity** | Converged MM-GBSA ΔG from ≥10 ns trajectory | **NOT available** — parser returns null, guard blocks writes |
| **Experimental validation** | MIC, MBC, hemolysis %, cytotoxicity | User-entered only; system never fabricates |

---

## 3. Boundary Enforcement Mechanisms

### 3.1 Code-Level Enforcement

- **MMPBSA Parser:** `official_mm_gbsa_delta_g` is hardcoded to `null` for all pilot/smoke data.
- **Persistence Guard:** Raises `PersistenceGuardError` if pilot data attempts to write official ΔG.
- **Database Schema:** `validation_status` defaults to `NOT_EXPERIMENTALLY_VALIDATED`.
- **Report Export:** Scientific boundary footer appears on every XLSX/PDF export.

### 3.2 UI-Level Enforcement

- **Orange banner:** "PILOT_ONLY_NOT_PRODUCTION_MMGBSA" on Readiness Card.
- **Null display:** "official ΔG: Not available" instead of a numeric value.
- **Warning boxes:** Explicit warnings for insufficient frames, short trajectories, unrealistic values.
- **Export footer:** "All scores are in-silico predictions. Wet-lab validation required before therapeutic claims."

### 3.3 Documentation-Level Enforcement

- Every release seal document reiterates boundaries.
- Teacher materials include assessment rubric on boundary awareness.
- Demo flow explicitly asks: "Why is official_mm_gbsa_delta_g null?"

---

## 4. Known Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| User misinterprets pilot MM-GBSA as real affinity | Medium | High | Orange banner, null display, mandatory warnings, teacher training |
| System bug writes pilot ΔG as official | Low | Critical | Parser null + Guard block + pytest coverage |
| User fabricates wet-lab data | External | High | State machine requires completed runs + PASS quality flags |
| Over-claim in publication | External | High | Export footer + documentation + review process |
| Platform used for human therapy without validation | External | Critical | Disclaimer: "For research and teaching only" |

---

## 5. Regulatory and Ethical Position

STAMP v1.0 is:
- **A research and teaching tool**, not a medical device.
- **Not FDA-approved**, not CE-marked, not validated for clinical use.
- **Not a substitute** for experimental validation, toxicology studies, or regulatory review.
- **Open-source** for transparency and community audit.

Any therapeutic claim based on STAMP outputs must be supported by:
1. Independent experimental validation (MIC, MBC, hemolysis, cytotoxicity, in-vivo efficacy).
2. Third-party structural validation (X-ray crystallography or cryo-EM).
3. Regulatory review (FDA, EMA, or national equivalent).
