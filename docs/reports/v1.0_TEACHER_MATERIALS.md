# STAMP v1.0 Teacher Materials

**Stage:** v1.0-P6  
**Audience:** University instructors, workshop trainers

---

## Learning Objectives

After this workshop, students will be able to:

1. Distinguish in-silico prediction from experimental validation.
2. Explain why pilot MM-GBSA results cannot rank candidates.
3. Navigate the STAMP pipeline end-to-end.
4. Interpret MMPBSA parser output and guard warnings.
5. Identify scientific boundary enforcement in code, tests, and UI.

## Hands-on Exercise

### Task
Load the demo project, run screening, and answer:

1. What is the `convergence_status`?
2. Why is `official_mm_gbsa_delta_g` null?
3. List three warnings from the Readiness Card.
4. What changes are needed for "production" status?

### Answers

1. `PILOT_ONLY_NOT_PRODUCTION_MMGBSA`
2. 100 ps trajectory with 7 frames is insufficient for converged binding free energy.
3. (a) Only 7/100 frames, (b) physically unrealistic ΔTOTAL, (c) G gas = 0.
4. ≥10 ns MD, proper sander energy terms, ≥90% frames, convergence diagnostics.
