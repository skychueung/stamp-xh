# STAMP v1.0 — 10-Minute Talk Script

**For:** Thesis defense, collaboration pitch, lab meeting  
**Speaker notes included**

---

## Slide 1: Title (30 sec)

> "STAMP v1.0: A Computational Platform for Targeted Anti-Infective Peptide Design"

**Script:**
"Today I'll introduce STAMP, a platform that bridges computational peptide design and experimental validation. This is the v1.0 demo release, ready for teaching and collaboration."

---

## Slide 2: The Problem (1 min)

> Antibiotic resistance + need for targeted therapeutics

**Script:**
"Antibiotic resistance is a global crisis. Broad-spectrum antibiotics damage microbiomes and select for resistance. Targeted antimicrobials — like STAMP hybrids — bind specific pathogens while sparing commensal bacteria. But designing them requires integrating epitope prediction, peptide generation, structure validation, and experimental testing."

---

## Slide 3: STAMP Architecture (1 min)

> 6-step pipeline diagram

**Script:**
"STAMP provides a complete workflow: from target protein input, through epitope screening and targeting peptide generation, to STAMP hybrid assembly, structure validation, MM-GBSA readiness checking, and finally wet-lab validation tracking. Each step is modular and auditable."

---

## Slide 4: What's New in v1.0 (1 min)

**Script:**
"v1.0 packages three major capabilities:
1. **v0.11 Wet-lab Validation** — Record MIC, MBC, hemolysis, cytotoxicity with a full state machine.
2. **v0.13 MM-GBSA Boundary Enforcement** — Parse binding free energy estimates while strictly separating pilot from production data.
3. **v1.0 Teacher-Ready Demo** — One-command startup, demo dataset, and complete teaching materials."

---

## Slide 5: Scientific Integrity (1.5 min)

> Code enforces boundaries, not just documentation

**Script:**
"A critical feature of STAMP is that scientific boundaries are enforced by code, not just documented. Our MMPBSA parser always sets `official_mm_gbsa_delta_g = null` for pilot data. The persistence guard blocks any attempt to write pilot results as production binding affinity. The frontend shows an orange 'PILOT_ONLY' banner. And all candidates default to `NOT_EXPERIMENTALLY_VALIDATED` until real wet-lab data is entered."
