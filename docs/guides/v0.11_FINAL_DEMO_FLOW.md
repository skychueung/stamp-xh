# STAMP v0.11 Final Demo Flow

This guide walks through the complete v0.11 wet-lab validation workflow from candidate selection to report export.

---

## Prerequisites

- A STAMP project with at least one computationally designed candidate (from v0.10).
- Logged-in user with access to the project.

---

## Step 1: Open FinalRankingPage

1. Navigate to **Projects** → select your project.
2. Go to **Final Ranking** tab.
3. Observe the ranked candidate list with `composite_score`, `mean_plddt`, `pdockq`, and `interaction_energy`.

> 💡 These are **computational predictions**, not experimental data.

---

## Step 2: Select a Candidate for Validation

1. Click on a candidate row (e.g., candidate with high composite_score and good pLDDT).
2. The candidate detail panel opens showing:
   - Sequence information
   - Computational metrics (pLDDT, pDockQ, FoldX)
   - **Validation Status**: `NOT_EXPERIMENTALLY_VALIDATED`

---

## Step 3: Create a Validation Run

1. In the candidate detail, click **"New Validation Run"**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Experiment Type**: `MIC`
   - **Organism**: `Staphylococcus aureus`
   - **Strain**: `MSSA`
   - **Protocol Name**: `CLSI M07-A10`
   - **Operator**: Your name
   - **Notes**: Initial screening
3. Click **Save**.
4. The run is created with status `PLANNED` and validation status `EXPERIMENT_PLANNED`.

---

## Step 4: Enter MIC / Hemolysis Measurements

1. Under the newly created run, click **"Add Measurement"**.
2. Enter first measurement:
   - **Metric**: `MIC_ug_ml`
   - **Value**: `2.5`
   - **Unit**: `ug/ml`
   - **Quality Flag**: `PASS`
   - **Replicate ID**: `R1`
3. Add second measurement (create a new HEMOLYSIS run first):
   - **Experiment Type**: `HEMOLYSIS`
   - **Metric**: `hemolysis_percent`
   - **Value**: `5.0`
   - **Unit**: `%`
   - **Quality Flag**: `PASS`
4. Observe that the candidate's **Validation Status** may update to `PARTIALLY_VALIDATED` automatically.

> ⚠️ All values must be real experimental results. The system does NOT generate them.

---

## Step 5: Open ExperimentalValidationDashboard

1. Navigate to **Experimental Validation** from the project sidebar.
2. The Dashboard shows:
   - **Total Candidates**: N
   - **With Measurements**: N (updated after your entries)
   - **Coverage**: X%
   - **Validation Status Distribution**: colored cards showing counts per status
   - **Measurement Counts**: breakdown by metric type
   - **Top Priority Candidates**: table with scores
3. Observe that your candidate now appears in the statistics.

---

## Step 6: Upload CSV Bulk Import

1. In the Dashboard, click **"Download Template"** to get a CSV template.
2. Fill in multiple rows with real measurement data for several candidates.
3. Click **"Upload CSV"** and select your file.
4. Review the import result:
   - Success count / total rows
   - Created runs and measurements
   - Any row-level errors
5. The Dashboard refreshes automatically with updated coverage.

---

## Step 7: Open CandidatePrioritizationPage

1. Click **"Candidate Prioritization"** button in the Dashboard header.
2. The Prioritization page shows:
   - Summary cards: Total, Ready, Needs Data, Safety Concern, Shortlisted
   - Filterable table with all candidates
   - Columns: Sequence, Composite Score, Exp Priority Score, Priority Status, Validation Status, MIC, Hemolysis, pLDDT, pDockQ, FoldX, Decision
3. Find your candidate:
   - If MIC + low hemolysis → `READY_FOR_REVIEW`
   - If high hemolysis → `SAFETY_CONCERN`
   - If no safety data → `NEEDS_MORE_DATA`

---

## Step 8: Set Manual Decision

1. Click on a candidate row to open the **Detail Drawer**.
2. Review the **Computational Metrics** panel (pLDDT, pDockQ, FoldX).
3. Review the **Experimental Measurements** panel (MIC, hemolysis).
4. In the **Manual Decision** form:
   - **Decision**: `SHORTLIST`
   - **Reason**: "Low MIC (2.5 ug/ml) and acceptable hemolysis (5%)."
   - **Reviewer**: Your name
   - **Notes**: "Prioritize for repeat assay and in-vivo testing."
5. Click **Save Decision**.
6. Observe:
   - The candidate's `priority_status` display updates to reflect the manual decision.
   - `composite_score` is UNCHANGED.
   - `validation_status` is UNCHANGED (still `PARTIALLY_VALIDATED`, NOT auto-promoted to `EXPERIMENTALLY_VALIDATED`).

---

## Step 9: Export Wet-lab Validation Report

1. Click **JSON**, **MD**, or **CSV** export button in the Prioritization page header.
2. The browser downloads:
   ```
   STAMP_wetlab_validation_report_{project_id}_{YYYYMMDD}.json
   STAMP_wetlab_validation_report_{project_id}_{YYYYMMDD}.md
   STAMP_wetlab_validation_report_{project_id}_{YYYYMMDD}.csv
   ```
3. Open the Markdown report and verify:
   - **Section 1 (Scientific Boundary)**: Explicitly states experimental data is user-entered.
   - **Section 4 (Validation Status)**: Your candidate appears with correct status.
   - **Section 5 (Measurements)**: MIC = 2.5, hemolysis = 5.0 are listed.
   - **Section 6 (Decisions)**: Your SHORTLIST decision appears with reason and reviewer.
   - **Section 7 (Computational Reference)**: pLDDT, pDockQ, FoldX are in a SEPARATE section with disclaimer.

---

## Step 10: Emphasize Computational vs Experimental Boundary

Throughout the demo, point out:

| Computational (v0.10) | Experimental (v0.11) |
|-----------------------|----------------------|
| composite_score | MIC_ug_ml, MBC_ug_ml |
| pLDDT | hemolysis_percent |
| pDockQ | cell_viability_percent |
| FoldX energy | quality_flag (PASS/WARNING/FAILED) |
| BepiPred3 / PepMLM | operator, protocol_name, experiment_date |

> 🔬 **Key message**: STAMP separates in-silico design from wet-lab validation. Computational scores guide which candidates to test, but only real experiments determine activity and safety.
