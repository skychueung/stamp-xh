## 4. Scientific Boundary

**All outputs are computational predictions, model-generated candidates, or computational energy/interface estimates. They are NOT experimentally validated.**

### Explicitly PROHIBITED declarations:
- ❌ "已验证抗菌活性" (experimentally verified antimicrobial activity)
- ❌ "已证明 MIC" (demonstrated MIC)
- ❌ "已证明低毒性" (demonstrated low toxicity)
- ❌ "已证明结合亲和力" (demonstrated binding affinity)
- ❌ `docking_score` (not calculated)
- ❌ `MM-GBSA ΔG` (not calculated)

### What IS present:
- ✅ BepiPred3 epitope prediction scores (computational)
- ✅ PepMLM-650M generated peptide sequences (model-generated)
- ✅ LocalColabFold structure predictions (computational, mean pLDDT / pTM)
- ✅ pDockQ interface quality estimates (computational)
- ✅ FoldX interaction_energy (computational, NOT docking_score or MM-GBSA ΔG)
- ✅ `validation_status: "NOT_EXPERIMENTALLY_VALIDATED"` on all candidates

## 6. Known Limitations

1. **尚未进行湿实验验证** — All candidate metrics are computational; no wet-lab validation has been performed.
2. **MIC / MBC 未接入真实实验数据** — No experimental MIC/MBC data is available or reported.
3. **溶血 / 毒性未接入真实实验数据** — No experimental hemolysis or toxicity data is available or reported.
4. **docking_score 未计算** — Traditional docking scores are not part of the current pipeline.
5. **MM-GBSA ΔG 未计算** — MM-GBSA binding free energy is not part of the current pipeline.
6. **FoldX interaction_energy ≠ docking_score ≠ MM-GBSA ΔG** — FoldX calculates empirical force-field based interaction energies for protein-protein complexes. It is explicitly NOT labeled as docking_score or MM-GBSA ΔG in any report or display.
7. **pDockQ 是界面计算置信度，不是实验亲和力** — pDockQ estimates the confidence of a predicted protein-protein interface. It is NOT an experimental binding affinity measurement.

## 7. Recommended v0.11 Direction

- **Wet-lab validation workflow** — Integration of experimental data entry and tracking
- **MIC / MBC experimental data entry** — Support for importing real antimicrobial assay results
- **Hemolysis / toxicity result management** — Support for importing cytotoxicity assay results
- **Candidate prioritization rules** — Rule-based or ML-based prioritization using both computational and experimental features
- **Experiment report template** — Wet-lab validated candidate reports with proper experimental attribution
- **Deployment hardening** — Production deployment, monitoring, logging, and error recovery
