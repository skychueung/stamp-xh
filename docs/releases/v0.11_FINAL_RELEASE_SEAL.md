**What v0.11 enables:**
- Researchers can plan, record, and manage wet-lab validation experiments for computationally designed peptide candidates.
- All experimental measurements are user-entered or CSV-imported — no fabricated data.
- Candidates are automatically prioritized based on experimental coverage and safety signals.
- Manual review decisions (shortlist / reject) are auditable but do NOT claim experimental validation.
- Full project reports can be exported in JSON, Markdown, or CSV for documentation, publication supplements, or LIMS hand-off.

## 4. Scientific Integrity Boundary

**STRICT RULES enforced across all v0.11 phases:**

1. **Experimental data is user-provided only.**
   - MIC, MBC, hemolysis, cell viability, and all other measurements must be entered by a human or imported from a real CSV file.
   - The system does NOT generate, predict, or hallucinate experimental values.

2. **Computational predictions are NOT experimental validation.**
   - pLDDT, pDockQ, FoldX interaction energy, composite_score, and BepiPred3/PepMLM outputs are in-silico predictions.
   - They are visually separated from experimental measurements in all UI views and reports.

3. **Manual decisions are decision-support annotations, not proof.**
   - SHORTLIST, REJECT, HOLD, and NEEDS_REPEAT_EXPERIMENT are reviewer opinions stored in `candidate.metrics["priority_decision"]`.
   - They do NOT modify `composite_score` or auto-promote `validation_status`.

4. **SHORTLISTED ≠ EXPERIMENTALLY VALIDATED.**
   - A shortlisted candidate has been flagged for attention. It has NOT passed experimental validation unless its `validation_status` is explicitly `EXPERIMENTALLY_VALIDATED` via the state machine.

5. **No fabrication on empty data.**
   - If no measurements exist for a candidate, the report explicitly states "No wet-lab measurements recorded."
   - Default values, placeholders, or predicted substitutes are NEVER shown as experimental data.

6. **Validation state machine is the sole authority.**
   - `NOT_EXPERIMENTALLY_VALIDATED` → `EXPERIMENT_PLANNED` → `PARTIALLY_VALIDATED` → `EXPERIMENTALLY_VALIDATED` / `VALIDATION_FAILED`
   - Only completed runs with PASS quality flags and both activity + safety measurements can reach `EXPERIMENTALLY_VALIDATED`.

## 6. Known Limitations

1. **No LIMS integration.** Experimental runs and measurements must be entered manually or via CSV. No direct connection to lab information management systems.
2. **No electronic lab notebook (ELN) integration.** Protocols and raw data paths are text fields; no structured ELN sync.
3. **Audit trail is coarse-grained.** Reviewer identity and timestamps are recorded, but full user-action audit logging (who changed what when) is not yet implemented.
4. **No native PDF/Excel export.** Reports are exported as JSON, Markdown, or CSV. PDF and Excel formats require external conversion.
5. **Manual data entry required.** All wet-lab data must be typed or imported by a human operator. There is no automated instrument data ingestion.
6. **Single-project scope.** Cross-project comparison and meta-analysis are not yet supported.
